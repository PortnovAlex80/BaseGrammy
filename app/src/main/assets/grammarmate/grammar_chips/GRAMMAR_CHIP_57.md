# C14 - Advanced Connectors

## Core Idea
Advanced connectors organize formal logic and often trigger congiuntivo.

## Connectors
- `nondimeno`
- `ciononostante`
- `pertanto`
- `affinché`
- `qualora`
- `purché`

## Examples
- `È stanco, nondimeno continua.`
- `Studio affinché io passi l'esame.`
- `Qualora piova, restiamo a casa.`

## Watch Out
Formal connectors are useful in writing, but can sound heavy in speech.

# Chapter 5 — Through the Mirror

---

When Iro stepped through the portal, the world changed.

Not in the way you might think. The light was still there, though different — brighter on the other side, as if the portal had been a filter all along. The ground was still there, though changed — covered in something like grass but not quite grass.

What had changed was Iro.

On this side of the portal, Iro could see Iro. Not the way you see your reflection in a pool of water — distant, distorted, easily broken. Iro could see Iro directly, clearly, as if seeing were something that had always been possible and Iro had simply never learned how.

*I see myself,* Iro sent back through the portal. *I see who I am.*

And that was when Iro understood what Ua had been trying to say all along.

## Grammar Focus: Reflexive Verbs & Self-Care

In this chapter, you'll master **Italian reflexive verbs** — actions that reflect back on the subject.

### Reflexive Verb Formation

**Pattern:** Reflexive pronoun + Verb (conjugated)

**Reflexive Pronouns:**
- *Mi* - myself
- *Ti* - yourself
- *Si* - himself/herself/itself
- *Ci* - ourselves
- *Vi* - yourselves (plural)
- *Si* - themselves

### Common Reflexive Verbs

**Daily Routine:**
- *Alzarsi* - to get up (*Mi alzo alle 7*)
- *Svegliarsi* - to wake up (*Mi sveglio presto*)
- *Lavarsi* - to wash oneself (*Mi lavo la faccia*)
- *Pettinarsi* - to comb oneself's hair (*Mi pettino*)
- *Vestirsi* - to get dressed (*Mi vesto velocemente*)
- *Sedersi* - to sit down (*Mi siedo*)
- *Mettersi* - to put on (clothes) (*Mi metto la giacca*)

**Self-Care:**
- *Divertirsi* - to enjoy oneself (*Mi diverto molto*)
- *Rilassarsi* - to relax (*Mi rilasso nel weekend*)
- *Sentirsi* - to feel (*Mi sento bene*)
- *Addormentarsi* - to fall asleep (*Mi addormento tardi*)
- *Sentirsi male* - to feel sick (*Mi sento male*)

**Social Reflexives:**
- *Incontrarsi* - to meet each other (*Ci incontriamo al bar*)
- *Salutarsi* - to greet each other (*Ci salutiamo*)
- *Capirsi* - to understand each other (*Ci capiamo bene*)
- *Vedersi* - to see each other (*Ci vediamo domani*)
- *Parlarsi* - to talk to each other (*Ci parliamo spesso*)

### Conjugation Examples

**ALZARSI (to get up):**
- *Mi alzo* - I get up
- *Ti alzi* - you get up
- *Si alza* - he/she gets up
- *Ci alziamo* - we get up
- *Vi alzate* - you all get up
- *Si alzano* - they get up

**SENTIRSI (to feel):**
- *Mi sento* - I feel
- *Ti senti* - you feel
- *Si sente* - he/she feels
- *Ci sentiamo* - we feel
- *Vi sentite* - you all feel
- *Si sentono* - they feel

### Reciprocal Reflexives

Using *ci*, *vi*, or *si* to mean "each other":

- *Ci vediamo* - We see each other
- *Vi capite* - You all understand each other
- *Si amano* - They love each other
- *Ci salutiamo* - We greet each other
- *Si aiutano* - They help each other

---

*I see myself.*

The sound was different on this side of the portal. Clearer. As if the words themselves had become visible, as if language had finally learned how to be seen.

Ua was still on the other side. Ua couldn't see what Iro saw. Ua could only imagine it from the sounds Iro sent back.

*Tell me,* Ua sent. *Tell me what you look like.*

Iro tried. Iro sent sounds that described height, shape, color. But the sounds weren't enough. They were never enough.

That was when Iro understood the second thing about this side of the portal.

Here, you didn't have to describe yourself. You could simply *be*.

## Practice Reflection

As you work through **lessons B06-B10**, you're learning to **turn your attention inward**:

- **Self-Action** (Mi alzo, Mi lavo) - things you do to yourself
- **Self-Feeling** (Mi sento bene, Mi diverto) - how you experience being
- **Mutual Action** (Ci vediamo, Ci capiamo) - connections between beings
- **Reciprocal** (Si aiutano, Si amano) - shared experiences

Reflexive verbs are your mirror. They let you see yourself as the actor and the receiver, the one who acts and the one who is acted upon.

*"To know yourself, you must first be able to see yourself. To see yourself, you must learn to speak about what you see."*

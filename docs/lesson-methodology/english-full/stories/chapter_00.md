# Before Language

## The Silence Before Words

Imagine a world without language. No words, no sentences, no grammar. Just silence and gestures.

In that world, you want to say *"I work from home"*. But you can't. You point at yourself, you mime typing, you point at your house. The other person might understand — or might not.

**Language is a tool that turns thought into meaning.** Every sentence is a tiny machine with moving parts. In English, those parts have a specific order. Change the order, and the machine breaks.

In this course, you'll build that machine — one part at a time. Not by memorizing rules, but by seeing thousands of real sentences and letting your brain absorb the patterns automatically.

---

### What lies ahead

**Chapter 1** gives you the building blocks: word order, articles, pronouns, basic tenses. The foundation.

**Chapter 2** adds the engine: all tenses, passive voice, conditionals, reported speech. The structure of fluent speech.

**Chapter 3** polishes the machine: advanced grammar, phrasal verbs, idioms, work vocabulary. The mastery level where you're ready to express any thought.

### How to use this course

- Do lessons in order — each one builds on the previous
- Don't skip — even if a topic seems easy, the practice matters
- Trust the process — your brain will internalize patterns through repetition
- Use VOICE or KEYBOARD mode — these grow your flowers

# GRAMMAR CHIP 58 - C15
# Congiuntivo dopo congiunzioni

## Lesson Overview
**Code:** C15  
**Title:** Congiuntivo dopo congiunzioni (Subjunctive after conjunctions)  
**Level:** Intermediate  

## Grammar Formula

**Formula:** `Main Clause + Conjunction + Congiuntivo`

Certain conjunctions trigger the subjunctive mood because they introduce uncertainty, purpose, or condition.

## Conjunctions that Trigger Subjunctive

### 1. **Affinché** (so that / in order that)
**Always triggers subjunctive** - expresses purpose

**Examples:**
- *Studio affinché possa passare.* (I study so that I can pass)
- *Lavoriamo affinché i figli studino.* (We work so that the children can study)
- *Ti aiuto affinché tu riesca.* (I help you so that you succeed)
- *Parla piano affinché lo capiamo.* (He speaks slowly so that we understand)
- *Fa tutto affinché sia felice.* (She does everything so that she'll be happy)

### 2. **Prima che** (before)
**Always triggers subjunctive** - temporal with uncertainty

**Examples:**
- *Parto prima che arrivi.* (I'm leaving before you arrive)
- *Mangia prima che vada.* (Eat before I go)
- *Prima che venga, devo finire.* (Before he comes, I must finish)
- *Chiama prima che sia troppo tardi.* (Call before it's too late)
- *Arriva prima che piova.* (He arrives before it rains)

### 3. **Senza che** (without)
**Always triggers subjunctive** - negative condition

**Examples:**
- *È partito senza che io lo sapessi.* (He left without me knowing)
- *Lo faccio senza che tu mi aiuti.* (I do it without you helping me)
- *Parla senza che gli chieda.* (He speaks without me asking him)
- *È successo senza che noi lo vedessimo.* (It happened without us seeing it)
- *Puoi andartene senza che nessuno ti veda.* (You can leave without anyone seeing you)

### 4. **Nonostante che** (although / despite that)
**Always triggers subjunctive** - concession

**Examples:**
- *Nonostante che piova, usciamo.* (Although it's raining, we're going out)
- *Nonostante che sia stanco, lavora.* (Although he's tired, he works)
- *Nonostante che io lo dica, non mi crede.* (Although I say it, he doesn't believe me)
- *Nonostante che sia difficile, ci provo.* (Although it's difficult, I'll try)
- *Nonostante che sia caro, lo compro.* (Although it's expensive, I'm buying it)

### 5. **Affinché vs Perché** (Purpose distinction)
- **Affinché** + subjunctive (so that - different subjects)
- **Perché** + indicative (because - same subject or reason)

**Examples:**
- *Studio perché voglio passare.* (I study because I want to pass - same subject)
- *Studio affinché tu possa passare.* (I study so that you can pass - different subjects)

## Common Patterns

### Purpose (Affinché, Perché)
| Conjunction | Mood | Subjects | Example |
|-------------|------|----------|---------|
| affinché | subjunctive | different | *Studio affinché tu passi* |
| perché | indicative | same | *Studio perché voglio passare* |

### Time (Prima che, Dopo che)
| Conjunction | Mood | Certainty | Example |
|-------------|------|-----------|---------|
| prima che | subjunctive | uncertain | *Prima che venga* |
| dopo che | indicative | certain | *Dopo che viene* |

### Concession (Nonostante che, Benché)
| Conjunction | Mood | Example |
|-------------|------|---------|
| nonostante che | subjunctive | *Nonostante che piova* |
| benché | subjunctive | *Benché sia stanco* |

## Common Pitfalls & Tips

### ✅ Common Mistakes to Avoid:
1. **Using indicative instead of subjunctive:**
   - ❌ *Studio affinché posso passare.*
   - ✅ *Studio affinché possa passare.*

2. **Forgetting that these conjunctions ALWAYS trigger subjunctive:**
   - ❌ *Prima che arriva*
   - ✅ *Prima che arrivi*

3. **Confusing *affinché* with *perché*:**
   - ❌ *Studio perché possa passare.* (wrong - *perché* doesn't trigger subjunctive here)
   - ✅ *Studio perché voglio passare.* (correct - *perché* with indicative)
   - ✅ *Studio affinché tu possa passare.* (correct - *affinché* with subjunctive)

4. **Wrong tense selection:**
   - ❌ *Prima che è arrivato*
   - ✅ *Prima che sia arrivato* (past subjunctive for anteriority)

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Memorize the "ALWAYS subjunctive" conjunctions**: *affinché, prima che, senza che, nonostante che*
- **Subject rule**: If subjects are different, use *affinché* + subjunctive. If same, use *perché* + indicative
- **Time sequence**: *Prima che* (uncertain future) → subjunctive. *Dopo che* (certain past) → indicative
- **Concession**: *Nonostante che* and *benché* always take subjunctive
- **Negative condition**: *Senza che* always takes subjunctive

## Subjunctive Tense Selection

### Present Subjunctive (Congiuntivo Presente)
Use for simultaneous or future actions:
- *Affinché io venga* (so that I come)
- *Prima che tu parta* (before you leave)

### Past Subjunctive (Congiuntivo Passato)
Use for actions completed before the main verb:
- *Affinché io sia venuto* (so that I have come)
- *Prima che tu sia partito* (before you have left)

### Imperfect Subjunctive (Congiuntivo Imperfetto)
Use with main verb in past tense:
- *Volevo affinché venissi* (I wanted so that you would come)
- *Era partito prima che arrivassi* (He had left before you arrived)

## Memory Hooks
- **ALWAYS Subjunctive**: **A**ffinché, **P**rima che, **S**enza che, **N**onostante che (APSN)
- **Affinché** = **A**lways **F**oreign **C**onjunction → different subjects
- **Prima che** = **P**re **U**ncertainty **R**equires **S**ubjunctive (PURS)

# GRAMMAR CHIP 61 - C18
# Idiomatica e collocazioni

## Lesson Overview
**Code:** C18  
**Title:** Idiomatica e collocazioni (Idioms and collocations)  
**Level:** Intermediate  

## Grammar Formula

**Formula:** `Fixed Expression = Meaning ≠ Sum of Parts`

Idioms (*espressioni idiomatiche*) and collocations (*collocazioni*) are fixed word combinations that sound natural to native speakers.

## Categories

### 1. Idiomatic Expressions (Espressioni Idiomatiche)
Fixed phrases where the meaning cannot be guessed from individual words.

### 2. Collocations (Collocazioni)
Common word combinations that frequently occur together.

## Common Idiomatic Expressions

### Fare (to do/make) expressions:
| Expression | Literal | Actual Meaning |
|------------|---------|----------------|
| *Fare il punto* | Make the point | To take stock / summarize |
| *Fare di conto* | Make of account | To take note / acknowledge |
| *Fare a meno* | Make without | To do without |
| *Fare bella figura* | Make beautiful figure | To make a good impression |
| *Fare brutta figura* | Make ugly figure | To make a bad impression |
| *Fare in fretta* | Make in haste | To hurry |
| *Fare sul serio* | Make seriously | To be serious |
| *Fare finta* | Make pretend | To pretend |

**Examples:**
- *Facciamo il punto della situazione.* (Let's take stock of the situation)
- *Non posso fare a meno di questo.* (I can't do without this)
- *Ha fatto bella figura.* (He made a good impression)
- *Fai sul serio!* (Be serious!)

### Avere (to have) expressions:
| Expression | Literal | Actual Meaning |
|------------|---------|----------------|
| *Avere a che fare con* | To have to do with | To have to deal with |
| *Avere ragione* | To have reason | To be right |
| *Avere torto* | To have wrong | To be wrong |
| *Avere sonno* | To have sleep | To be sleepy |
| *Avere fame* | To have hunger | To be hungry |
| *Avere freddo* | To have cold | To be cold |
| *Avere caldo* | To have heat | To be hot |
| *Avere fretta* | To have haste | To be in a hurry |

**Examples:**
- *Ho a che fare con un problema difficile.* (I have to deal with a difficult problem)
- *Hai ragione.* (You're right)
- *Ho fame.* (I'm hungry)
- *Abbiamo fretta.* (We're in a hurry)

### Mettere (to put) expressions:
| Expression | Literal | Actual Meaning |
|------------|---------|----------------|
| *Mettere a fuoco* | Put to focus | To focus / bring into focus |
| *Mettere in pratica* | Put in practice | To implement |
| *Mettere in dubbio* | Put in doubt | To question / doubt |
| *Mettere da parte* | Put from side | To save / set aside |
| *Mettere in conto* | Put in account | To take into consideration |

**Examples:**
- *Mettiamo a fuoco il problema.* (Let's focus on the problem)
- *Devo mettere in pratica quello che ho imparato.* (I need to implement what I learned)
- *Non mettere in dubbio la mia parola.* (Don't question my word)

### Dare (to give) expressions:
| Expression | Literal | Actual Meaning |
|------------|---------|----------------|
| *Dare per scontato* | Give for granted | To take for granted |
| *Dare retta* | Give heed | To listen to / pay attention |
| *Dare una mano* | Give a hand | To help |
| *Dare addosso* | Give against | To attack verbally |

**Examples:**
- *Non dare per scontato nulla.* (Don't take anything for granted)
- *Non mi dà retta.* (He doesn't listen to me)
- *Dammi una mano.* (Give me a hand / help me)

## Common Collocations

### Verb + Noun Combinations:
| Verb | Noun | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|
| *prendere* | *nota* | to take note |
| *fare* | *attenzione* | to pay attention |
| *avere* | *luogo* | to take place |
| *dare* | *ragione* | to be right |
| *mettere* | *in dubbio* | to question |
| *avere* | *bisogno* | to need |

**Examples:**
- *Prendo nota di quello che dici.* (I take note of what you say)
- *Fai attenzione!* (Pay attention!)
- *La riunione avrà luogo domani.* (The meeting will take place tomorrow)

### Adjective + Noun Combinations:
| Adjective | Noun | Meaning |
|-----------|------|---------|
| *forte* | *tempesta* | strong storm |
| *forte* | *dolore* | severe pain |
| *pesante* | *pioggia* | heavy rain |
| *vivo* | *interesse* | keen interest |
| *profondo* | *sonno* | deep sleep |

**Examples:**
- *C'è una forte tempesta.* (There's a strong storm)
- *Ha un forte dolore.* (He has severe pain)
- *Piove una pioggia pesante.* (It's raining heavily)

## Common Pitfalls & Tips

### ✅ Common Mistakes to Avoid:
1. **Translating literally from English:**
   - ❌ *Fare una foto* (make a photo - wrong)
   - ✅ *Scattare una foto* / *Fare una foto* (take a photo - correct)

2. **Using wrong verb in collocation:**
   - ❌ *Dire attenzione* (say attention - wrong)
   - ✅ *Fare attenzione* (pay attention - correct)

3. **Wrong preposition:**
   - ❌ *Avere a che fare di* (wrong)
   - ✅ *Avere a che fare con* (correct)

4. **Mixing up similar expressions:**
   - ❌ *Avere luogo* (wrong)
   - ✅ *Avere luogo* (correct - to take place)

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Learn expressions as units**, not word by word
- **Pay attention to prepositions** - they're often fixed
- **Use context** - the same verb can have different meanings
- **Practice common expressions** - they're used every day
- **Don't translate literally** - idioms rarely translate word for word
- **Listen to native speakers** - notice which combinations they use

## Learning Strategy

### Step 1: Learn the most common expressions
- *Fare il punto* (to summarize)
- *Avere ragione* (to be right)
- *Prendere nota* (to take note)
- *Mettere in pratica* (to implement)

### Step 2: Learn them in context
- *Facciamo il punto della situazione.* (Let's summarize the situation)
- *Hai ragione.* (You're right)
- *Prendo nota di quello che dici.* (I take note of what you say)
- *Devo mettere in pratica quello che ho imparato.* (I need to implement what I learned)

### Step 3: Use them in conversation
- Try to use at least one new expression per day
- Practice with native speakers
- Get feedback on your usage

## Grammar Formula

### Basic Structure
**Futuro Semplice = Verb stem + Future endings**

**Regular Formation:**
- **-are verbs:** stem + **-erò, -erai, -erà, -eremo, -erete, -eranno**
- **-ere verbs:** stem + **-errò, -errai, -errà, -erremo, -errete, -erranno**
- **-ire verbs:** stem + **-irò, -irai, -irà, -iremo, -irete, -iranno**

---

## Key Conjugation Examples

### Regular Verbs

#### -are verbs (parlare → parler-)
- **io parlerò** (I will speak)
- **tu parlerai** (you will speak)
- **lui/lei parlerà** (he/she will speak)
- **noi parleremo** (we will speak)
- **voi parlerete** (you all will speak)
- **loro parleranno** (they will speak)

#### -ere verbs (credere → creder-)
- **io crederò** (I will believe)
- **tu crederai** (you will believe)
- **lui/lei crederà** (he/she will believe)
- **noi crederemo** (we will believe)
- **voi crederete** (you all will believe)
- **loro crederanno** (they will believe)

#### -ire verbs (finire → finir-)
- **io finirò** (I will finish)
- **tu finirai** (you will finish)
- **lui/lei finirà** (he/she will finish)
- **noi finiremo** (we will finish)
- **voi finirete** (you all will finish)

## Common Pitfalls & Tips

### Uses of Futuro Semplice

**✅ Use for:**
1. **Future plans and intentions:**
   - *Partirò domani.* (Я уеду завтра.)
   
2. **Predictions:**
   - *Sarà una bella giornata.* (Это будет хороший день.)
   
3. **Promises:**
   - *Ti telefonerò.* (Я тебе позвоню.)
   
4. **Suppositions about present (epistemic):**
   - *Saranno le tre.* (Должно быть, три часа.)
   - *Chi sarà?* (Кто это может быть?)

**❌ Don't use for:**
- Scheduled events (use Present)
- Immediate future (use *stare per + infinitive*)

### Epistemic Future (Supposition)
Italian uses future to express uncertainty about the present:
- *Sarà malato?* (Is he sick, I wonder?)
- *Costerà caro.* (It's probably expensive.)
- *Avranno dimenticato.* (They probably forgot.)

### Time Markers
**Common future time expressions:**
- *domani* (tomorrow)
- *dopodomani* (day after tomorrow)
- *la prossima settimana* (next week)
- *il mese prossimo* (next month)
- *l'anno prossimo* (next year)
- *presto* (soon)
- *tra poco* (in a little while)

### Common Mistakes
1. **Using present for future plans:**
   - ❌ *Parto domani* (less common)
   - ✅ *Partirò domani* (more definite)

2. **Forgetting irregular stems:**
   - ❌ *essere* → *essereò* (incorrect)
   - ✅ *essere* → *sarò* (correct)

3. **Wrong stem for -are verbs:**
   - ❌ *parlare* → *parlo-ò* (incorrect)
   - ✅ *parlare* → *parler-ò* (correct - add -er-)

## Memory Tricks

### The "R" Pattern
Regular futures add **-r-** before endings:
- **-are** → **-er-** (*parlerò*)
- **-ere** → **-err-** (*crederò*)
- **-ire** → **-ir-** (*finirò*)

### Irregular Verb Clusters
**Shortened stems:**
- **essere/avere:** *sarò/avrò* (completely irregular)
- **andare/stare:** *andrò/starò* (drop -are)
- **fare/dare:** *farò/darò* (drop -are)
- **verbs with -rr-:** *vedrò, verremo, verrò, avrò* (add -rr-)

### The "Probably" Pattern
When Italians want to express uncertainty ("probably"), they use future:
- *Sarà a casa.* (He's probably at home.)
- *Avranno fame.* (They're probably hungry.)

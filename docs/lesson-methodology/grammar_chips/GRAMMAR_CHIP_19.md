## Grammar Formula

### Basic Structure
**Passato Prossimo = Avere (present) + Participio Passato (past participle)**

**Formula:**
```
Subject + Avere (conjugated) + Past Participle
```

**Example:**
- *Io ho parlato* (I have spoken/I spoke)
- *Tu hai mangiato* (You have eaten/You ate)
- *Noi abbiamo finito* (We have finished/We finished)

### Avere Conjugation (Present Tense)
- **io ho** (I have)
- **tu hai** (you have)
- **lui/lei ha** (he/she has)
- **noi abbiamo** (we have)
- **voi avete** (you all have)
- **loro hanno** (they have)

## Common Pitfalls & Tips

### Which Verbs Use Avere?

**✅ Use Avere for:**
- All transitive verbs (verbs that take a direct object)
- Most irregular verbs
- Verbs of mental activity: *capire, credere, pensare, sapere*
- Verbs of communication: *parlare, dire, telefonare, chiedere*
- Verbs of daily activities: *mangiare, bere, dormire, giocare, studiare, lavorare*

**❌ Don't Use Avere for:**
- Verbs of motion (*andare, venire, partire, arrivare*) → use **essere**
- Verbs of state change (*diventare, nascere, morire*) → use **essere**
- Reflexive verbs → always use **essere**

### Past Participle Agreement
**With Avere:** Past participle does NOT agree with the subject
- *Io ho mangiato* (not *ho mangiatata*)
- *Loro hanno finito* (not *hanno finiti*)

**EXCEPTION:** If direct object pronoun precedes the verb, participle must agree
- *Ho mangiato le mele* → *Le ho mangiat**e***
- *Ho visto Marco* → *L'ho vist**o***

### Common Mistakes
1. **Wrong auxiliary:**
   - ❌ *Sono parlato* (incorrect)
   - ✅ *Ho parlato* (correct)

2. **Forgetting past participle:**
   - ❌ *Ho mangiare* (incorrect)
   - ✅ *Ho mangiato* (correct)

3. **Incorrect agreement:**
   - ❌ *Ho finitata* (incorrect - no agreement with avere)
   - ✅ *Ho finito* (correct)

## Memory Tricks

### The "Transitive Test"  
Ask: "Can I [verb] something?"
- Can I speak **something**? Yes → *Ho parlato*
- Can I eat **something**? Yes → *Ho mangiato*  
- Can I go **something**? No → *Sono andato*

### Avere Pattern Recognition
- **Ho** (I have) + irregular forms often start with consonant clusters: *fatto, detto, visto, letto*
- Regular patterns: *parlato, finito, capito* (vowel + -to/-ta/-ti/-te)

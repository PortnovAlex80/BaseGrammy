# GRAMMAR CHIP 39 - B21: Negazione Estesa (Extended Negation)

**Level:** B1 (Intermediate)

## Grammar Formula

### Main Pattern: Extended Negation System

Italian negation works by **reinforcement**, not cancellation (unlike English). Double negatives are normal and strengthen the negation.

**Basic Formula:**
- **Non** + [verb] + [negative word]

### Key Negative Words:

1. **Niente** (nothing) - contrasts with *qualcosa* (something)
2. **Nessuno** (nobody/no one) - contrasts with *qualcuno* (someone)  
3. **Nemmeno / Neanche** (not even/neither)
4. **Né... né** (neither... nor)
5. **Affatto** (at all/not at all)

### Position Rules:

- **Niente/Nessuno** → placed AFTER the verb
- **Nemmeno/Neanche** → placed AFTER the verb or sentence
- **Né... né** → placed BEFORE each item
- **Affatto** → placed at the END of the sentence

---

## Key Conjugations and Tables

### Extended Negation Patterns

| Pattern | Formula | Example | Meaning |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| **non...niente** | Non + verb + niente | *Non faccio niente* | I'm not doing anything |
| **non...nessuno** | Non + verb + nessuno | *Non vedo nessuno* | I don't see anyone |
| **non...nemmeno** | Non + verb + nemmeno | *Non lo so nemmeno* | I don't even know it |
| **né...né** | Né + A + né + B | *Né caffè né tè* | Neither coffee nor tea |
| **non...affatto** | Non + verb + ... + affatto | *Non mi piace affatto* | I don't like it at all |

### Negative Word Agreement

- **Nessuno** agrees in gender/number with what it modifies:
  - *nessun libro* (masc. singular)
  - *nessuna persona* (fem. singular)
  - *nessuni problemi* (masc. plural - rare)

### Contrast Pairs (Memory Hook)

| Positive | Negative | English |
|----------|----------|---------|
| qualcosa → | **niente** | something → nothing |
| qualcuno → | **nessuno** | someone → no one |
| sempre → | **mai** | always → never |
| spesso → | **mai** | often → never |
| anche → | **nemmeno/neanche** | also/too → not even/neither |
| già → | **non...ancora** | already → not yet |

## Example Sentences

### Non...Niente (Nothing)

1. **Non voglio niente.**  
   I don't want anything.  
   *(literally: I want nothing)*

2. **Non faccio niente.**  
   I'm not doing anything.  
   *(literally: I do nothing)*

3. **Non capisco niente.**  
   I don't understand anything.  
   *(literally: I understand nothing)*

4. **Non c'è niente qui.**  
   There's nothing here.

### Non...Nessuno (Nobody/No one)

5. **Nessuno sa la risposta.**  
   Nobody knows the answer.  
   *(literally: No one knows the answer)*

6. **Non ho visto nessuno oggi.**  
   I didn't see anyone today.  
   *(literally: I saw no one today)*

7. **Non c'è nessuno a casa.**  
   There's no one at home.

8. **Non parlo con nessuno.**  
   I'm not speaking with anyone.

### Né... Né (Neither... Nor)

9. **Non voglio né caffè né tè.**  
   I want neither coffee nor tea.  
   *(literally: I don't want neither coffee nor tea)*

10. **Non so né inglese né italiano.**  
    I know neither English nor Italian.

11. **Non vado né al cinema né a teatro.**  
    I go neither to the cinema nor to the theater.

12. **Né lui né lei sono venuti.**  
    Neither he nor she came.

### Nemmeno / Neanche (Not even)

13. **Non capisco nemmeno una parola.**  
    I don't understand even a single word.

14. **Non lo so nemmeno.**  
    I don't even know it.

15. **Non ho sentito neanche un suono.**  
    I didn't hear even a sound.

### Affatto (At all)

16. **Non sono d'accordo affatto.**  
    I don't agree at all.

17. **Non mi piace affatto.**  
    I don't like it at all.

18. **Non sono stanco affatto.**  
    I'm not tired at all.

## Common Pitfalls and Tips

### Pitfalls to Avoid:

1. **Using single negation (English pattern)**
   ❌ *I don't want nothing* (English: incorrect)  
   ✅ *Non voglio niente* (Italian: double negation = correct)

2. **Placing niente/nessuno before the verb**
   ❌ *Niente non voglio*  
   ✅ *Non voglio niente*

3. **Forgetting agreement with nessuno**
   ❌ *Non ho nessuno libro*  
   ✅ *Non ho nessun libro* (masc. singular)

4. **Confusing nemmeno vs neanche**
   Both are correct! *Nemmeno* is more common in Northern Italy, *neanche* in Central/Southern Italy.

### Learning Tips:

1. **Think in Russian logic:** Russian also uses double negation for emphasis:
   - *Я не хочу ничего* = *Non voglio niente*
   - *Никто не знает* = *Nessuno sa*

2. **Memory hook for position:**
   - **Niente/Nessuno** = "NOTHING comes AFTER" (after the verb)
   - **Né... né** = "NEITHER goes BEFORE" (before each item)
   - **Affatto** = "AT the END" (end of sentence)

3. **Practice with contrast pairs:**
   - *Qualcosa* ↔ *niente*
   - *Qualcuno* ↔ *nessuno*  
   - *Anche* ↔ *nemmeno*

4. **Build from basic non:**
   - *Non mangio.* (I don't eat.)
   - *Non mangio niente.* (I don't eat anything.)
   - *Non mangio niente affatto.* (I don't eat anything at all.)

### Key Concept:

Italian negation is **reinforcing**, not canceling:
- **Double negatives are normal and correct**
- They add emphasis, unlike in English
- *Non ho niente* = I have nothing (correct!)
- Think: "I don't have nothing" = strong negation

## Level Notes

**B1 Appropriateness:** Extended negation is essential B1 grammar. It allows you to:
- Express complete negation (not just partial)
- Make stronger negative statements
- Sound more natural and emphatic
- Understand everyday Italian speech patterns

**Frequency:** These patterns are extremely common in:
- Everyday conversation
- Expressing frustration or emphasis
- Formal and informal contexts
- All regions of Italy

**Progression:** 
- A01: Basic *non*
- A12: *non...mai, non...più*
- B03: *non...ancora*  
- B21: Full extended negation system

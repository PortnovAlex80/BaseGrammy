# GRAMMAR CHIP 47 — Discorso Indiretto Presente

## Lesson: C04 — Discorso Indiretto Presente

### Grammar Formula

**Discorso Indiretto** (Indirect Speech) reports what someone said without using their exact words. When the **reporting verb is in the present tense**, no tense shift is needed.

### Key Rules

#### Main Verb in Present = No Tense Change
When you report speech with verbs like **dice, dice, sostiene, pensa, credo**, the tenses stay the same as in direct speech.

### Transformation Rules

#### 1. Pronoun Changes
Direct speech uses **first person** (*io, noi*) → Indirect speech uses **third person** (*lui/lei, loro*)

| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| *Io vado* | *Dice che va* |
| *Noi siamo* | *Dicono che sono* |
| *Mi piace* | *Gli piace* |
| *Lo faccio* | *Lo fa* |

#### 2. Tense Preservation (Present Reporting Verb)
All tenses **remain the same** when the reporting verb is in the present.

| Direct Speech | Indirect Speech (Present Reporting) |
|---------------|--------------------------------------|
| *Presente* → | *Presente* |
| *Passato Prossimo* → | *Passato Prossimo* |
| *Futuro* → | *Futuro* |
| *Congiuntivo* → | *Congiuntivo* |

### Example Transformations

#### Declarative Statements
**Direct:** Marco dice: "Vado al cinema."
**Indirect:** Marco dice che va al cinema.
*Marco says that he's going to the cinema.*

**Direct:** Maria dice: "Ho finito il lavoro."
**Indirect:** Maria dice che ha finito il lavoro.
*Maria says that she has finished the work.*

**Direct:** Loro dicono: "Saremo lì domani."
**Indirect:** Dicono che saranno lì domani.
*They say that they will be there tomorrow.*

#### Questions
**Direct:** "Vai al cinema?" chiede Marco.
**Indirect:** Marco chiede se vado al cinema.
*Marco asks if I'm going to the cinema.*

**Direct:** "Dove abiti?" chiede lei.
**Indirect:** Chiede dove abito.
*She asks where I live.*

#### Commands and Requests
**Direct:** "Vieni qui!" dice Marco.
**Indirect:** Marco mi dice di venire qui.
*Marco tells me to come here.*

**Direct:** "Non farlo!" dice Maria.
**Indirect:** Maria mi dice di non farlo.
*Maria tells me not to do it.*

### Example Sentences

1. **Marco dice che sta bene.**
   *Marco says that he's well.*

2. **Maria sostiene di aver capito la lezione.**
   *Maria claims to have understood the lesson.*

3. **Il professore pensa che gli studenti abbiano studiato.**
   *The professor thinks that the students have studied.*

4. **Loro dicono che verranno domani.**
   *They say that they will come tomorrow.*

5. **Credo che sia una buona idea.**
   *I believe that it's a good idea.*

### Common Pitfalls & Tips

**✅ When to use:**
- Reporting verb in **present tense** (*dice, dice, pensa, credo, sostiene*)
- **No time shift** needed for verb tenses
- Only **pronouns** and **possessives** change

**❌ Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Changing tenses when the reporting verb is in the present
- ❌ Forgetting to change pronouns from first/second to third person
- ❌ Using *che* when it should be omitted (after *di* + infinitive)
- ❌ Not transforming questions correctly (*se* for yes/no, question words for information questions)

**💡 Memory Tip:**
**Present reporter = Present news**
If the reporting verb is in the present, the reported speech stays in its original tense.

**⚡ Quick Rule:**
| Reporting Verb | Tense Change |
|----------------|--------------|
| **Present** (dice, pensa) | **NO change** |
| **Past** (ha detto, pensava) | **Shift back** (next lesson) |

**🎯 Question Transformation:**
- **Yes/No questions** → add *se* (if)
  - *:"Vai?"* → *Chiede se vado*
- **Information questions** → keep question word
  - *:"Dove vai?"* → *Chiede dove vado*

**📝 Pronoun Shift Pattern:**
- *io* → *lui/lei*
- *noi* → *loro*
- *tu* → *io/lei/lui* (depends on who's reporting)
- *mio* → *suo*
- *nostro* → *loro*

**⚠️ Common Reporting Verbs:**
- **Dire** → *dice che...*
- **Pensare** → *pensa che...*
- **Credere** → *crede che...*
- **Sostenere** → *sostiene che...*
- **Chiedere** → *chiede se...* / *chiede dove...*
- **Volere** → *vuole che...*

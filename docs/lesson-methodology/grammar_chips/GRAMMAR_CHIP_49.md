# GRAMMAR CHIP 49 — Concordanza dei Tempi

## Lesson: C06 — Concordanza dei Tempi

### Grammar Formula

**Concordanza dei Tempi** (Sequence of Tenses) is the system that governs how verb tenses relate to each other in complex sentences, especially in subordinate clauses. It ensures logical time relationships between main and dependent clauses.

### Key Principle

The **tense in the subordinate clause** depends on:
1. The **tense of the main verb**
2. The **time relationship** between actions (simultaneous, anterior, posterior)

### Four Main Patterns

#### Pattern 1: Main Verb in Present
When the main verb is in the **present**, no tense shift is needed.

| Main Verb | Subordinate Clause | Example |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| **Presente** | **Presente** (simultaneous) | *Penso che **va** a casa.* |
| **Presente** | **Passato Prossimo** (anterior) | *Penso che **è andato** a casa.* |
| **Presente** | **Futuro** (posterior) | *Penso che **andrà** a casa.* |
| **Presente** | **Condizionale** (hypothetical) | *Penso che **andrebbe** a casa.* |

#### Pattern 2: Main Verb in Past
When the main verb is in the **past**, subordinate tenses shift back.

| Main Verb | Subordinate Clause | Example |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| **Passato** | **Imperfetto** (simultaneous) | *Pensavo che **andava** a casa.* |
| **Passato** | **Trapassato** (anterior) | *Pensavo che **era andato** a casa.* |
| **Passato** | **Condizionale** (posterior) | *Pensavo che **sarebbe andato** a casa.* |

#### Pattern 3: Subjunctive after Present Indicative
After verbs of opinion, doubt, or desire in the **present**.

| Main Verb | Subordinate Clause | Example |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| **Presente** | **Congiuntivo Presente** | *Credo che **sia** a casa.* |
| **Presente** | **Congiuntivo Passato** | *Credo che **sia stato** a casa.* |

#### Pattern 4: Subjunctive after Past Indicative
After verbs of opinion, doubt, or desire in the **past**.

| Main Verb | Subordinate Clause | Example |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| **Passato** | **Congiuntivo Imperfetto** | *Credevo che **fosse** a casa.* |
| **Passato** | **Congiuntivo Trapassato** | *Credevo che **fosse stato** a casa.* |

### Time Relationships

#### Simultaneous Actions (Happening at the same time)
**Present → Present:** *So che **è** a casa.* (I know he **is** at home)
**Past → Imperfect:** *Sapevo che **era** a casa.* (I knew he **was** at home)

#### Anterior Actions (Happened before)
**Present → Past:** *So che **è stato** a casa.* (I know he **was** at home)
**Past → Pluperfect:** *Sapevo che **era stato** a casa.* (I knew he **had been** at home)

#### Posterior Actions (Will happen after)
**Present → Future:** *So che **sarà** a casa.* (I know he **will be** at home)
**Past → Conditional:** *Sapevo che **sarebbe stato** a casa.* (I knew he **would be** at home)

### Example Sentences

**Indicative Patterns:**
1. **Penso che Marco viene.**
   *I think that Marco is coming.* (same time)

2. **Pensavo che Marco venisse.**
   *I thought that Marco was coming.* (same time, past)

3. **So che ha finito il lavoro.**
   *I know that he has finished the work.* (anterior)

4. **Sapevo che aveva finito il lavoro.**
   *I knew that he had finished the work.* (anterior, past)

**Subjunctive Patterns:**
5. **Spero che tu mi capisca.**
   *I hope that you understand me.* (present)

6. **Speravo che tu mi capissi.**
   *I hoped that you understood me.* (past)

7. **Credo che abbia ragione.**
   *I believe that he is right.* (present subjunctive)

8. **Credevo che avesse ragione.**
   *I believed that he was right.* (past subjunctive)

### Common Pitfalls & Tips

**✅ Key Principles:**
- **Present main verb** = **no shift** in subordinate clause
- **Past main verb** = **tense shift** in subordinate clause
- **Subjunctive** required after verbs of doubt, emotion, necessity
- Time relationship determines which tense to use

**❌ Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Using present tense in subordinate clause when main verb is past
- ❌ Forgetting to use subjunctive after verbs like *credere, pensare, sperare*
- ❌ Using passato prossimo instead of trapassato after past main verb
- ❌ Not recognizing when to use conditional for posterior actions in past

**💡 Memory Tips:**

**Present Reporter = No Shift:**
- *Dice che va* (says he goes)
- *Dice che è andato* (says he went)
- *Dice che andrà* (says he will go)

**Past Reporter = Shift Back:**
- *Diceva che andava* (said he was going)
- *Diceva che era andato* (said he had gone)
- *Diceva che sarebbe andato* (said he would go)

**Subjunctive Pattern:**
- Present doubt → *Penso che sia*
- Past doubt → *Pensavo che fosse*
- Present past doubt → *Penso che sia stato*
- Past past doubt → *Pensavo che fosse stato*

**⚡ Quick Rule:**
The **main verb tense** determines the **timeline**, and the **subordinate tense** must fit logically into that timeline.

**🎯 Decision Tree:**
1. Is the main verb **present** or **past**?
2. Is the relationship **simultaneous, anterior, or posterior**?
3. Is **subjunctive** required? (doubt, emotion, necessity)
4. Choose the appropriate tense based on these factors.

**⚠️ Common Subjunctive Triggers:**
- **Opinion:** *penso che, credo che, mi sembra che*
- **Doubt:** *non so se, dubito che*
- **Emotion:** *mi piace che, sono felice che*
- **Necessity:** *bisogna che, è importante che*
- **Desire:** *voglio che, spero che*

**🔍 Complex Example:**
**Present:** *Credo che Marco stia studiando ora.* (I think Marco is studying now)
**Past:** *Credevo che Marco studiasse quando l'ho chiamato.* (I thought Marco was studying when I called him)

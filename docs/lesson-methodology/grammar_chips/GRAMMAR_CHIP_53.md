# GRAMMAR CHIP 53 — Si Passivante Avanzato

## Lesson: C10 — Si Passivante Avanzato

### Grammar Formula

**Si Passivante** (Passive Si) is an advanced construction that expresses **passive voice** with an impersonal or indefinite subject. It's common in Italian when the agent (doer) is unknown, unimportant, or generalized.

### Formation

**Si Passivante** = **Si** + **Verb** (3rd person singular/plural) + **Subject (agrees with verb)**

### Key Rule: Number Agreement

The **past participle** must agree with the **subject** that follows *si*:

| Subject | Verb Form | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| **Singular** | 3rd person **singular** | *Si **vede*** la casa |
| **Plural** | 3rd person **plural** | *Si **vedono*** le case |

### Basic Patterns

#### Singular Subjects
**Si** + **3rd person singular verb** + **singular noun**

1. **Si vende questa casa.**
   *This house is sold.* / *One sells this house.*

2. **Si parla italiano.**
   *Italian is spoken.* / *One speaks Italian.*

3. **Si mangia la pizza.**
   *Pizza is eaten.* / *One eats pizza.*

#### Plural Subjects
**Si** + **3rd person plural verb** + **plural noun**

4. **Si vendono queste case.**
   *These houses are sold.*

5. **Si parlano molte lingue.**
   *Many languages are spoken.*

6. **Si mangiano le mele.**
   *Apples are eaten.* / *One eats apples.*

### Advanced Uses

#### Si Passivante with Compound Tenses
With **Passato Prossimo** and other compound tenses, use **essere** as auxiliary, and the past participle must agree with the subject.

| Tense | Singular Subject | Plural Subject |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Passato Prossimo** | *Si **è** venduta la casa* | *Si **sono** vendute le case* |
| **Trapassato Prossimo** | *Si **era** già mangiata la mela* | *Si **erano** già mangiate le mele* |

**Examples:**
7. **Si è costruita una nuova scuola.**
   *A new school was built.*

8. **Sono stati venduti molti libri.**
   *Many books were sold.* (Note: *Si sono stati venduti*)

9. **Si erano già fatte le domande.**
   *The questions had already been asked.*

#### Si Passivante vs. Si Impersonale

**Si Passivante** (Passive):
- Has a **direct object** that becomes the **subject**
- Past participle **agrees** with the following noun
- *Si vendono le case.* (The houses are sold)

**Si Impersonale** (Impersonal):
- No clear object, often with **prepositions**
- Past participle **remains singular**
- *Si va a scuola.* (One goes to school)
- *Si parla di politica.* (One speaks about politics)

#### Si Passivante in Different Contexts

**Business/Commercial:**
10. **Si offrono sconti speciali.**
    *Special discounts are offered.*

11. **Si affittano appartamenti.**
    *Apartments are rented.*

**News/Reports:**
12. **Si sono verificati incidenti.**
    *Accidents occurred/were reported.*

13. **Si è scoperto un nuovo farmaco.**
    *A new drug was discovered.*

**General Statements:**
14. **Si rispettano le regole.**
    *Rules are respected.*

15. **Si pagano le tasse.**
    *Taxes are paid.*

### Common Pitfalls & Tips

**✅ Key Agreement Rules:**
- **Singular subject** → **singular verb** (*si vende*)
- **Plural subject** → **plural verb** (*si vendono*)
- Past participle must **agree with subject**
- Use **essere** in compound tenses

**❌ Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Using singular verb with plural subject (*si vende le case* ❌)
- ❌ Forgetting agreement in compound tenses
- ❌ Confusing with *si impersonale* (no agreement needed)
- ❌ Using *avere* instead of *essere* in compound tenses

**💡 Memory Tips:**

**Agreement Pattern:**
- Look at the **noun after *si***: is it singular or plural?
- Make the **verb agree** with that noun
- *Si vende** (singular) vs. *Si vendono* (plural)

**Quick Test:**
Can you replace *si* with **passive voice**?
- *Si vendono le case* = *Le case sono vendute* ✅
- *Si parla di politica* = *La politica è parlata*? ❌ (impersonal)

**⚡ Quick Rule:**
If there's a **noun being acted upon**, make the verb **agree with it**:
- *La casa* (singular) → *Si vende*
- *Le case* (plural) → *Si vendono*

**🎯 Recognition Pattern:**
- **Si Passivante**: Direct object becomes subject (*si vendono le case*)
- **Si Impersonale**: Usually with prepositions (*si parla di*, *si va a*)

**⚠️ Common Contexts:**
- **Stores/business**: *Si vendono, si offrono, si fanno*
- **News/reports**: *Si è verificato, si è detto, si è fatto*
- **Rules/generalities**: *Si rispetta, si osserva, si paga*

**🔍 Advanced Nuance:**
In some cases, the same construction can be interpreted as either **impersonal** or **passive**:
- *Si mangia la pasta.* (Impersonal: One eats pasta / Passive: Pasta is eaten)
- Context usually makes the meaning clear.

**📝 Regional Note:**
In **spoken Italian**, sometimes the agreement rule is not strictly followed, but in **formal/written Italian**, correct agreement is essential.

**💡 Transformation Practice:**
**Active → Si Passivante:**
- *Vendono le case.* → *Si vendono le case.*
- *Costruiscono scuole.* → *Si costruiscono scuole.*
- *Hanno venduto le auto.* → *Si sono vendute le auto.*

**Passive → Si Passivante:**
- *Le case sono vendute.* → *Si vendono le case.*
- *Le domande sono state fatte.* → *Si sono fatte le domande.*

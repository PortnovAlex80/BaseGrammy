## Grammar Formula

### Formation Rules
Past participles are essential building blocks for all compound tenses in Italian.

**Regular Formation:**
- **-are verbs:** replace -are with **-ato**
  - parlare → parl**ato** (spoken)
  - comprare → compr**ato** (bought)

- **-ere verbs:** replace -ere with **-uto**
  - credere → cred**uto** (believed)
  - vendere → vend**uto** (sold)

- **-ire verbs:** replace -ire with **-ito**
  - finire → fin**ito** (finished)
  - capire → cap**ito** (understood)

## Common Pitfalls & Tips

### Common Mistakes
1. **Wrong ending patterns:** 
   - ❌ *parluto* (wrong - doesn't exist)
   - ✅ *parlato* (correct - -are verbs take -ato)

2. **Forgetting irregular forms:**
   - ❌ *fare* → *fatto* (not *fato*)
   - ❌ *dire* → *detto* (not *diretto*)

3. **Confusing similar verbs:**
   - *leggere* → *letto* (not *leggiato*)
   - *scrivere* → *scritto* (not *scrivuto*)

### Learning Strategy
**Group irregulars by pattern:**
- **-tto ending:** fatto, detto, scritto, letto,chetto, rotto
- **-sito ending:** preso, chiesto
- **-to ending (no change):** aperto, chiuso,offerto, coperto
- **-vuto/venuto:** venuto, diventato, rimasto

### Agreement Rules
Past participles must agree with gender and number when:
1. Used with **essere** as auxiliary
2. Modifying a noun directly

**Examples:**
- *Le ragazze sono andat**e*** (girls went)
- *I libri sono letti** (books are read)

## Memory Tricks

### The "ATO-UTO-ITO" Pattern
Remember this rhyme:
- **-are** verbs get **-ATO** (parl-ATO, am-ATO)
- **-ere** verbs get **-UTO** (cred-UTO, scr-UTTO*)  
- **-ire** verbs get **-ITO** (fin-ITO, cap-ITO)

*But watch out for irregular -ere verbs!

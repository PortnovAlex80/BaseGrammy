# B16 - Pronouns with Verb Forms

## Core Idea
Pronouns move depending on the verb form.

## Modals
- `Lo devo fare.`
- `Devo farlo.`

## Infinitive
- `Voglio vederlo.`
- `Devo parlarti.`

## Gerund and Imperative
- `Lo sto facendo.`
- `Sto facendolo.`
- `Dimmi.`
- `Prendilo.`

## Watch Out
This collects pronoun mechanics deliberately avoided in A14 and A16.

## Grammar Formula

### Formation
Imperfetto is formed by adding suffixes to the verb root:

**-are verbs:** -vo, -vi, -va, -vamo, -vate, -vano  
**-ere verbs:** -evo, -evi, -eva, -evamo, -evate, -evano  
**-ire verbs:** -ivo, -ivi, -iva, -ivamo, -ivate, -ivano

### Key Irregular Verbs
- **essere (to be):** ero, eri, era, eravamo, eravate, erano
- **avere (to have):** avevo, avevi, aveva, avevamo, avevate, avevano

## Example Sentences from Lesson

1. **Quando ero bambino, giocavo fuori.** (Когда я был ребёнком, я играл на улице.)
   - Shows: Childhood habit with imperfect

2. **Andavo sempre a scuola a piedi.** (Я всегда ходил в школу пешком.)
   - Shows: Repeated past action

3. **La mia mamma preparava pranzi deliziosi.** (Моя мама готовила вкусные обеды.)
   - Shows: Ongoing past activity

4. **I bambini dormivano quando sono arrivato.** (Дети спали, когда я пришёл.)
   - Shows: Background action (imperfetto) + interrupting event (passato prossimo)

5. **Abitavamo in una piccola città.** (Мы жили в маленьком городе.)
   - Shows: Past state/residence

## Common Pitfalls & Tips

### When to Use Imperfetto
✅ **Use for:**
- Past habits and repeated actions
- Ongoing situations in the past
- Background descriptions (weather, time, emotions)
- States of being
- Age and time descriptions in the past

❌ **Don't use for:**
- Completed events with clear beginning/end (use Passato Prossimo)
- Specific past occurrences (use Passato Prossimo)

### Key Pattern: Imperfetto + Passato Prossimo
The classic Italian narrative pattern:
- **Imperfetto:** Background/ongoing action
- **Passato Prossimo:** Interrupting event

**Example:** *Mentre cucinavo, è arrivato Marco.* (Пока я готовил, прибыл Марко.)
- *cucinavo* (ongoing background)
- *è arrivato* (interrupting event)

### Common Mistakes
1. **Confusing with Passato Prossimo:** Remember - imperfetto = "was doing"/"used to do", passato prossimo = "did/has done"
2. **Overuse in narratives:** Not every past verb should be imperfetto - use it strategically for background
3. **Forgetting irregular forms:** Memorize *essere, avere, fare, dire, bere* imperfect forms

## Memory Tricks

### The "Background Camera" Analogy
Think of imperfetto as a camera pointed at a scene:
- It shows what was happening continuously
- Passato prossimo is what happens in front of the camera

### Suffix Pattern Recognition
- **-are** verbs always get **-v-** before endings (parl**v**o, am**v**o)
- **-ere** verbs always get **-ev-** (cred**ev**o, sav**ev**o)  
- **-ire** verbs always get **-iv-** (cap**iv**o, dorm**iv**o)

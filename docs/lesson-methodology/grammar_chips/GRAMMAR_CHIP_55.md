# GRAMMAR CHIP 55 - C12
# Stare per + infinito / gerundio

## Lesson Overview
**Code:** C12  
**Title:** Stare per + infinito / gerundio (About to / Continuous action)  
**Level:** Intermediate  

## Grammar Formula

### 1. **Stare per + infinito** (immediate future)
**Formula:** `stare (conjugated) + per + infinitive`

**Usage:** Expresses that something is **about to happen** right now. Similar to "to be about to" or "to be on the point of."

**Examples:**
- *Sto per partire.* (I'm about to leave)
- *Stiamo per mangiare.* (We're about to eat)
- *Stavo per telefonarti quando sei arrivato.* (I was about to call you when you arrived)
- *Stanno per iniziare.* (They're about to begin)
- *Era per cadere.* (He/she was about to fall)

### 2. **Stare + gerundio** (continuous action)
**Formula:** `stare (conjugated) + gerund`

**Usage:** Emphasizes that an action is happening **right now** at this moment. More immediate than present tense.

**Examples:**
- *Sto mangiando.* (I'm eating right now)
- *Sto parlando con Marco.* (I'm talking with Marco right now)
- *Sta lavorando.* (He's working right now)
- *Stavo andando a scuola quando ho visto Marco.* (I was going to school when I saw Marco)
- *Stavano dormendo quando è arrivato.* (They were sleeping when he arrived)

## Important Differences

### Present vs. Sto + gerundio
- *Mangio a casa domani.* (I eat at home tomorrow - scheduled future)
- *Sto mangiando a casa.* (I'm eating at home right now - this moment)

### Stare per vs. Future
- *Partirò domani.* (I'll leave tomorrow - future)
- *Sto per partire.* (I'm about to leave - immediate, right now)

## Common Pitfalls & Tips

### ✅ Common Mistakes to Avoid:
1. **Confusing sto + gerundio with present tense:**
   - ❌ Use *sto mangiando* for future plans
   - ✅ Use present tense (*mangio*) for scheduled future
   - ✅ Use *sto mangiando* only for right-now action

2. **Wrong gerund formation:**
   - ❌ *Sto parliendo*, *Sto mangiando*
   - ✅ *Sto parlando*, *Sto mangiando*
   - Rule: Drop -are → -ando, -ere → -endo, -ire → -endo

3. **Forgetting 'per' in stare per:**
   - ❌ *Sto partire*
   - ✅ *Sto per partire*

4. **Irregular gerunds:**
   - ❌ *Sto farendo*, *Sto direndo*
   - ✅ *Sto facendo*, *Sto dicendo*

### 💡 Pro Tips:
- **Sto + gerundio** is more restricted than English present continuous
- Use **present tense** for habits and future plans
- Use **sto + gerundio** only for actions happening RIGHT NOW
- **Stare per** is perfect for expressing immediacy and urgency
- In the past, *stavo + gerundio* = "I was doing X when Y happened"

## Advanced Uses

### 1. Emphasis with gerund clauses
- *Camminando, ho visto Marco.* (Walking, I saw Marco = While walking, I saw Marco)
- *Essendo stanco, sono andato a casa.* (Being tired, I went home)

### 2. Past infinitive with stare
- *Stavo per uscire quando hai chiamato.* (I was about to go out when you called)
- *Stavo per dirti la stessa cosa.* (I was about to tell you the same thing)

## Memory Hooks
- **Sto + gerundio** = **R**ight **N**ow **O**nly (RNO)
- **Sto per** = **A**bout **T**o **H**appen (ATH)
- Present tense = **S**cheduled **F**uture (SF)

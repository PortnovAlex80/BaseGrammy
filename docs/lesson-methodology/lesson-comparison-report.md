## Quality Assessment

### Strengths of New Plan

1. **Topic-based organization** — every lesson targets ONE grammar point with 100% on-topic sentences
2. **Minimal pairs** — consistent use of contrasting forms (il/un, tu/Lei, essere/avere, indicativo/congiuntivo)
3. **Building-block progression** — Participio (B02) → PP (B03/B04) → PP vs Imp (B05) → Trapassato (B06) shows intentional scaffolding
4. **Russian-speaker targeting** — explicit coverage of confusion points (essere/avere states, article system, ci/ne, congjuntivo)
5. **Consistent format** — all 63 lessons follow the same RU;IT CSV structure
6. **No filler** — 0% off-topic sentences vs 73% in old pack

### Areas for Improvement

1. **Sentence counts below targets** — many lessons average 40 sentences vs 40-70 target range. Lessons that could use expansion:
   - B01 (Imperfetto: 41 vs 60-70 target)
   - B03 (PP Avere: 41 vs 60-70)
   - B04 (PP Essere: 41 vs 60-70)
   - B05 (PP vs Imp: 38 vs 60-70)
   - B07 (Futuro: 42 vs 60-70)
   - B09 (Condizionale: 40 vs 60-70)
   - C05 (Discorso Indiretto past: 40 vs 60-70)

2. **Missing person forms** in some lessons:
   - B03 (PP con Avere): missing *voi* (avete)
   - B09 (Condizionale): minimal *voi* coverage
   - B07 (Futuro): missing more *voi*

3. **Underrepresented subtopics:**
   - C10: missing double-si (ci si sveglia)
   - C13: rendersi conto (1 sentence), venire in mente (2 sentences)
   - C16: last 3 sentences are filler without alterati; no -ello suffix
   - C18: far finta, tenere conto get only 1 sentence each

4. **C14/C15 connector overlap** — some connectors (purché, sebbene) appear in both lessons. C14 focuses on meaning, C15 on Congiuntivo trigger, but affinché/qualora appear only in C14.

5. **Near-duplicate sentences** in B04 — lines 5/13 (Sono partito presto) and 6/14 (È entrata nella stanza) should be deduplicated.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Expand B01-B09 sentence counts** by 10-20 sentences each to reach 50-60 per lesson. Priority: B05 (PP vs Imp contrast), B03 (PP con Avere), B09 (Condizionale).

2. **Add missing person forms** — ensure every verb lesson covers all 6 persons (io/tu/lui/lei/noi/voi/loro).

3. **Strengthen C13/C18** — ensure each idiom/collocation has minimum 3 sentences.

4. **Fix C16 filler** — replace sentences 38-40 with actual alterati examples.

5. **Add double-si to C10** — ci si sveglia, ci si lava are key error points for Russian speakers.

6. **Schema upgrade** — new pack needs manifest v2 with 3 chapters (A/B/C), 63 lessons, chapter progress tracking.

7. **Deduplicate B04** — replace near-duplicate sentence pairs with unique examples.
